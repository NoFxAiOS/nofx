Rule interpretation: SMA20/60, Wilder ATR14 seeded with the first 14 true ranges; first TR=high−low. E1–E4 priority assigns overlapping entries; X1–X4 priority then E4's SMA20 TARGET assigns exits. Windows of 3/5/10/20 bars include today; previous-five lows and previous-20 closing highs exclude today. New highs are strict closing highs; ties do not reset the clock. E4's key bar is close > previous high OR a bullish body engulfing the previous bearish body. X4 uses actual entry open minus 2×the last ATR known at entry (signal-close ATR). All MA/ATR windows are execution bars, including in 1h mode.

## 5. Execution and caveats

- Survivorship bias: universe is currently listed bStock names, or the explicitly labelled cached registry/subset; delisted names and historical listing changes are unavailable. Missing/short-history ranks remain unavailable, not backfilled.
- Yahoo daily adjusted OHLC can differ from bStock prices, dividends, splits, sessions and liquidity; hourly Yahoo data may have different adjustment treatment. Sources are disclosed above. No LLM layer is backtested: MOM_TOP is the mechanical close>SMA200, 12−1 momentum proxy (close[t−21]/close[t−252]−1).
- Signals use close t; daily fills use next SPY date's open, hourly fills use next SPY bar's open. Missing opens expire that order, with no deferred execution or replacement; missing close marks hold the last price. Entry events missed for lack of slots are not queued. A held exit that fails to fill needs a subsequent rule signal. All timestamps are UTC bar labels (open timestamps), while decisions/marks represent those bars' closes. Exact hourly timestamp alignment is required across data sources.
- Active slots target 1/maxpos equity at entry; fewer names leave cash at 0%. Buys are cash-capped after costs, fractional shares allowed, no leverage. Momentum rebalances solve equal post-cost target weights, selling dropouts and reweighting retained names. Missing holdings can constrain weights/slots. SPY_BH invests all cash in SPY; EW_BH is the all-name benchmark exception to maxpos, fixes names present at the initial signal close and rebalances every 63 execution bars.
- MA_CROSS enters only on an observed golden cross after the common start (no initial trend position). MOM_X_MA selects the momentum top N first, then vetoes names without SMA20>SMA60; no backfill, death-cross exits between rebalances, cash stays idle until the next rebalance.
- 1h mode requests 3000 bars and accepts shorter histories; Yahoo caps 60m history at roughly 730 days (~2 years), and 3000 requested bars can cover less. Hourly momentum uses prior-day daily bars only, including at the final hourly close. Momentum rebalances on every 10-th SPY trading day's last bar, starting at the common signal date. Costs exclude spread/slippage beyond the configured bps, taxes, funding and lot-size constraints.
